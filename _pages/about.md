My name is **Diego Olguín-Wende**. I hold a degree in Mathematical Engineering and a MSc in Applied Mathematics from the University of Chile. I am currently a **Research Engineer** in the Digital Health research line at the Center for Mathematical Modeling, University of Chile.

My research focuses on the use of **Data Science**, **Machine Learning**, and **Artificial Intelligence** in **healthcare** and **public health**. Beyond these areas, I have also applied methods from **Statistics**, **Optimization**, **Optimal Control**, **Differential Equations**, and **Operations Research** to challenges in healthcare, as well as to problems related to climate change.

I am also highly passionate about teaching, with experience in subjects such as Machine Learning, Statistics, Optimization, Optimal Control, and Differential Equations.
