My name is Diego Olguín-Wende, I'm a Mathematical Engineering and MSc in Applied Mathematics from University of Chile. I'm currently a Research Engineering from the Digital Health research line of Center of Mathematical Modeling of University of Chile. 

My main interests are Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence applied to healthcare and public health, but also I've worked with Statistics, Optimization, Optimal Control, Differential Equations and Operation Research tools to healthcare, public health and climate change.

I'm also so passionate for teaching in different areas like Machine Learning, Statistics, Optimization, Optimal Control and Differential Equations.
